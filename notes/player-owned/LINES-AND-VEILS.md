# Lines and veils

The player's, and **durable across campaigns** — which is why this lives here
and not in `notes/gm/plans/CAMPAIGN.md`. It ships with the template, so a new
campaign inherits it already settled and nobody has to have the conversation
again.

- **Lines** stay out entirely.
- **Veils** can exist, but happen off-page.

**Settled. Never asked about at the start of a session, and never negotiated
mid-scene.** Where you're unsure whether something falls under one of these,
take the more cautious reading. The player changes this by editing the file, or
by saying so in play — write it down when they do.

---

- **Lines:** none recorded
- **Veils:** none recorded

---

"None recorded" is the setting, not an unanswered question. The live safety
mechanisms don't depend on anything being written here: the player can stop or
rewind anything at any moment, and the GM checks in before and after a scene
that goes somewhere heavy.

**If you copied this template from someone else,** these are the values they
shipped. Replace them with your own — this file is the one piece of the
scaffolding that is about you rather than about the game.
