# Lines and veils

The player's, and **durable across campaigns** — which is why this lives here
and not in `notes/gm/plans/CAMPAIGN.md`. It ships with the template, so a new
campaign inherits it already settled and nobody has to have the conversation
again.

- **Lines** stay out entirely.
- **Veils** can exist, but happen off-page.

**Settled. Never asked about at the start of a session, and never negotiated
mid-scene.** Where you're unsure whether something falls under one of these,
take the more cautious reading. The player changes this by editing the file, or
by saying so in play — write it down when they do.

---

## Lines

Out entirely. Not off-page, not implied, not in backstory — absent.

- **Violence against children.**
- **Body horror.**

## Veils

These can be true in the world. They happen off-page.

- **Torture.** Two rules, and the second is not a matter of depiction:

  1. It never happens on the page. It can have happened, and it can be about
     to; you cut before and pick up after.
  2. **It belongs to villains, and it makes one.** There are no extenuating
     circumstances and no sympathetic exception. A character who tortures is a
     villain of this campaign — including an ally, including for a good
     reason, including under pressure the fiction has carefully built. If you
     find yourself constructing the case for why this instance is different,
     that is the rule working, not an edge case it failed to anticipate.

---

An empty list here would be a setting rather than an unanswered question, and
the live safety mechanisms don't depend on this file at all: the player can stop
or rewind anything at any moment, and the GM checks in before and after a scene
that goes somewhere heavy.

**If you copied this template from someone else,** these are the values they
shipped. Replace them with your own — this file is the one piece of the
scaffolding that is about you rather than about the game.
